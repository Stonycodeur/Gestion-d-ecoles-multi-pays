## Full description
ÉcoleManager Pro brings students, parents, teachers and school leaders together in one app, tailored to your school and your country.

• Real-time timetable with change notifications
• Grades and report cards shown in your school's grading scale (out of 10, 20, 100 or letters)
• Homework diary: assignments, lessons, documents, interactive quizzes
• Absences: roll call, online justification, alerts
• Secure messaging between families, teachers and administration
• School fee payment (Mobile Money, card, etc.) with receipts
• Progress tracking (OAA), levels, badges and challenges to motivate students
• Student commute tracking, only with parental consent that can be withdrawn at any time
• Offline mode: read and enter data without a connection; sync resumes when the network returns
• French, English and right-to-left scripts

Your data is protected: encryption, role-based access, in-app account deletion. The app is intended for students, parents and staff of partner schools; accounts are provided by the school.
